## The gate you can't miss

**Bab Demnat** — Gate 13 — is the souk's great south entrance: the widest opening, the busiest taxi drop-off, and the unofficial front door of [Africa's largest urban market](/en/history/from-talborjt-to-amsernat). If you arrive without a plan, you arrive here.

## Why start here

- **Orientation.** From the outside, the wall runs long and legible; from inside, everything is alleys. Start at the big gate, note its taxi rank, and you have a compass pin for the whole visit.
- **Access.** Petit taxis know it by name; the drop-off is at the wall itself.
- **The wings nearby.** The southern stretch leans food-and-household — butchers, [fish](/en/visit/gate-11-bab-tiznit-fish), kitchenware — before the lanes deepen toward the craft quarters.

## The anchor method

Whatever gate you enter, do this: **remember the gate's number, not just its name.** Thirteen gates named after cities from Fes lore to Timbuktu is a memory game; thirteen numbers on a wall is a system. Lost? Walk the wall — every gate leads out, and Gate 13 is the biggest.
