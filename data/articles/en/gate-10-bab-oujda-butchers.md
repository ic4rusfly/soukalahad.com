## The souk before it dresses for tourists

**Bab Oujda** — Gate 10 — is the butcher and poultry wing: the souk feeding itself since the 1960s, decades before anyone wrote a guide about it. Come at 8:00 and you'll see the real economy of [the market](/en/history/from-talborjt-to-amsernat): delivery mopeds, trolleys, restaurant buyers, families shopping for the day, everything at working pace.

## Is this for you?

If you're cooking — self-catering apartments around Agadir make this genuinely useful, and the quality is high and the prices are locals' prices, per kilo, effectively fixed. If you're not cooking, walk it anyway: ten minutes here recalibrates what you think this place is. It is not a theme park with a wall around it. It is a city organ.

## Practical notes

- Morning is the hour; by mid-afternoon the wing is winding down.
- No bargaining on meat prices — scales and rates are posted by the market's rhythm, not by theatre.
- Photography here asks for more courtesy than anywhere else in the souk. Ask, and accept no for an answer.

Nearby: [Gate 11 — the fish market](/en/visit/gate-11-bab-tiznit-fish), Agadir's other great food gate.
