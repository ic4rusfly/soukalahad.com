## Accept the maths first

Souk Al Ahad holds roughly **6,000 shops behind thirteen gates**. You cannot "do" it. What you can do is walk one intelligent loop through its five most rewarding wings in about two hours, which is exactly what this plan is: a loop, not a marathon. (Context: [why the souk is even here](/en/history/from-talborjt-to-amsernat).)

## Before you leave the hotel

- **Go in the morning.** The souk runs Tuesday to Sunday, roughly 9:00 to 19:00–21:00 depending on the season — **closed Mondays**. Mornings are cooler, calmer, and the light for photos is better.
- **Cash.** Cards are near-useless inside; the ATM queue at the gates is the slowest line in Agadir. Bring small notes.
- **Taxi protocol.** Orange petit taxis, up to three passengers, metered — insist politely: *"Le compteur, s'il vous plaît."* A ride from the hotel zones costs about **15–30 MAD**. Say **"Souk Al Had"** — not just "souk" — or a helpful driver may deliver you to a tourist trap instead.

## The loop (2 hours)

1. **Enter at Gate 2 — Bab Taroudant** (produce). Ten minutes of what the souk really is: crates, mint, juice presses. Drink the juice. Buy nothing yet.
2. **Gate 1 — Bab Tombouctou** (spices, olives). Buy the ras el hanout and the olives now — small, cheap, weigh nothing. See [the spice guide](/en/visit/gate-1-bab-tombouctou-spices).
3. **Gate 3 — Bab Sijilmassa** (pottery, lanterns). Browse, tap the tagines, note prices. Delay buying to the way back.
4. **Gate 4 — Bab Tamdoult** (carpets). The full ceremony — tea included — happens if you show real interest. Enjoy it. Decide your ceiling before you sit down. See [the carpet guide](/en/visit/gate-4-bab-tamdoult-carpets).
5. **Gate 5 — Bab Igli** (leather). The classic souvenirs live here: babouches, a bag, a belt. [How to spot real leather](/en/visit/gate-5-bab-igli-leather).
6. **Return past Gate 3** — now that you know the prices, buy the tagine or lantern you marked in hour one.

## Rules of dignity

- You are expected to bargain on crafts — it's the custom, not a fight. [The short version of the ritual](/en/visit/bargaining-101).
- Say no thanks with a smile and keep walking; it works.
- Photograph people, not just pyramids — after asking. A small purchase buys a lot of goodwill.
- Lost? Every gate leads out. The wall is your compass.

**When you're hooked:** come back on a Sunday, when the souk triples in size and energy, and see the parts you missed — the [argan and soap wing at Gate 9](/en/visit/argan-oil-real-or-fake) is the next essential stop.
