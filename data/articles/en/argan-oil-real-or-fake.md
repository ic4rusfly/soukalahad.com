## Liquid gold, and the industry of fake gold

Argan oil grows in one place on earth — the Souss, the region you are standing in when you stand in Agadir. That rarity created a counterfeit industry. The good news: the fakes are easy to beat with four tests and one piece of knowledge — **the price ladder**.

## The price ladder (at the source, 2026)

- **Culinary argan (roasted):** ~80–150 MAD per 100ml
- **Cosmetic argan (unroasted):** ~100–150 MAD per 100ml
- **Amlou** (argan + almonds + honey): ~60–100 MAD per 250g jar
- **Under ~50 MAD per 100ml:** assume diluted, refined, or not argan at all

Cooperatives sell at fixed prices without theatre. In the souk's argan wing — around **Gate 9, Bab Imouzzar** — you can find honest sellers and [the bargaining ritual](/en/visit/bargaining-101), but the price ladder still rules: a wildly cheap "argan" is not a bargain, it's a different liquid.

## The four tests

1. **Smell.** Culinary argan smells of roasted nuts. Cosmetic is mild, faintly nutty, never perfumed. A strong perfume means masked dilution; a neutral nothing on a "culinary" bottle means refined or substituted oil.
2. **Colour.** Culinary: deep amber. Cosmetic: light gold. Colourless "argan" is the loudest lie in the market.
3. **Clarity.** Slight natural sediment can occur in unfiltered culinary oil, but cloudiness or floating particles in cosmetic oil means poor processing.
4. **Absorption.** A drop of cosmetic argon on your wrist should absorb in a minute or two, light and dry, not greasy.

## Labels and bottles

One ingredient — *Argania spinosa* kernel oil. Dark glass, never clear plastic: light destroys argan. Batch, origin and date on the label are signs of a producer, not a mixer.

## Two last things

- Culinary and cosmetic are different products from different kernels. Cooking with cosmetic oil is a waste; putting roasted oil on your hair is a scent decision.
- Flying home: pack bottles in your checked luggage, wrapped in socks. It's oil; pressure and baggage handlers are not its friends.

Buy it at the right gate, test it once, and it's still a third of the European shop price. That's the whole secret.
