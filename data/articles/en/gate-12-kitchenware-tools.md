## The wing without a postcard — with the best prices

Gate 12 is the least photographed and most useful wing of the souk: tools, kitchenware, enamelware, aluminium stockpots, gas burners, and every object a Moroccan kitchen actually runs on. Nothing here is staged for visitors, which is exactly why the prices are the souk's most honest.

## What to buy here

- **The real cooking tagine.** Unglazed terracotta, made for fire — this is where cooks buy them, at a fraction of the price of the [decorative pieces at Gate 3](/en/visit/gate-3-bab-sijilmassa-pottery). Season it before first use: soak, dry, oil lightly.
- **The couscoussier** — the two-tier steamer that rolls, sprays and steams every Friday in the country. Aluminium versions here cost less than a pizza in Europe.
- **Teapots and trays** — from workhorse aluminium to engraved brass.
- **The mortar** — the heavy granite or brass one that actually makes chermoula, not the souvenir-sized one.

## Good to know

- Light bargaining — a discount, not a duel. See [the rules](/en/visit/bargaining-101).
- This is the wing where "shipped vs. suitcase" decisions get made: a couscoussier is suitcase-shaped; six stockpots are a shipment.
- Buy the tagine here, buy the decorative bowl at Gate 3 — locals do exactly that.
