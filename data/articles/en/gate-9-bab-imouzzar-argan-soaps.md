## The apothecary gate

**Bab Imouzzar** — named for the mountain town of Imouzzar Ida Outanane — opens the souk's apothecary wing: argan oil, amlou, black soap, ghassoul clay, rose water, and the whole hammam arsenal. This is the gate that connects the souk to the Souss countryside around it — [the region where argan grows and nowhere else on earth](/en/visit/argan-oil-real-or-fake).

## The short version of not getting burned

The full method — the price ladder (80–150 MAD per 100ml at source, under 50 means fake) and the four tests (smell, colour, clarity, absorption) — lives in the [argan guide](/en/visit/argan-oil-real-or-fake). Read it before this gate, not after.

## Beyond argan

- **Savon noir** — the black olive-based soap of the hammam. Cheap, honest, sold by weight.
- **Ghassoul** — the mineral clay that Moroccan hair-washing was built on. A few dirhams for a block that lasts months.
- **Rose water** — ideally from Kelaat M'Gouna, the valley of roses; check the label for origin, not just poetry.
- **Amlou** — argan, almonds, honey. [The price ladder](/en/visit/argan-oil-real-or-fake) applies: around 60–100 MAD per 250g jar is honest.

Prices here float with [bargaining](/en/visit/bargaining-101), but the honest band is narrow — the genuine article has a floor.
