## The gate that breaks the postcard

**Bab Tanger** — Gate 8 — is the souk's electronics wing: phone shops, repair counters, accessories, lamps, gadgets and the unapologetic commerce of 2026. Visitors expecting only carpets and spice pyramids find this wing disorienting. Locals find it indispensable — this is where Agadir's phones get fixed and its chargers get replaced.

## Why it's cheaper

No brand-store rent, no official distribution margins, and parallel imports. A cable, a case, a pair of earbuds here costs a fraction of the mall price — and the repair counters fix what the brand stores replace.

## The one rule

**If a known-brand item is priced far below its official price, it is grey-market, refurbished, or counterfeit.** There is no fourth option. That is not always a dealbreaker — a 30 MAD charging cable you treat as disposable is honest commerce — but a "brand-new flagship phone" at half price is none of those things. For anything with a battery, buy from a shop that will remember you, and test everything at the counter before paying.

## Good to know

- Haggling is mild here — expect a small discount, not the [full ritual](/en/visit/bargaining-101).
- Memory cards, "AirPods-style" earbuds and smartwatch lookalikes are the wing's three classic tourist traps. Decide what you're buying: a cheap gadget, or a real product. Both exist here.
