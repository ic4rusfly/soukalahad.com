## The day, in order

**8:45 — Enter at Bab Demnat.** The big south gate, the wall, the first wall of sound. Grab juice and m'semen at the produce wing ([Gate 2](/en/visit/gate-2-bab-taroudant-produce)) — breakfast costs less than the tip at a hotel.

**9:15 — The two-hour loop.** Spices at [Gate 1](/en/visit/gate-1-bab-tombouctou-spices), pottery at [Gate 3](/en/visit/gate-3-bab-sijilmassa-pottery), the carpet ceremony at [Gate 4](/en/visit/gate-4-bab-tamdoult-carpets), leather at [Gate 5](/en/visit/gate-5-bab-igli-leather). The full route is [the first-visit plan](/en/visit/first-visit-two-hour-plan); go Tuesday–Thursday if you can ([crowd science](/en/visit/sunday-vs-weekday-crowds)).

**11:30 — Sardines at Gate 11.** The charcoal lunch where the workers eat — [30–50 MAD](/en/visit/what-things-cost-price-list) for the best meal in Agadir. ([Gate 11](/en/visit/gate-11-bab-tiznit-fish))

**13:00 — The beach and the promenade.** Ten minutes by petit taxi to the corniche. Agadir's bay is a crescent of calm Atlantic and the city's modern face — walk it off, or sit at a café and watch the surf schools.

**15:30 — Choose your afternoon:**
- **The marina** for gelato and boats, or
- **Medina d'Agadir (Polizzi)** — a hand-built recreation of a traditional medina, 20 minutes south — or
- **Taghazout**, if surfing is why you came.

**17:30 — Up the Oufella hill.** Cable car from the marina side or a short drive: the restored kasbah wall above the port, and the terrace where the old city died. Read first: [15 seconds that erased a city](/en/history/agadir-earthquake-1960) — the hill is a memorial, and knowing why changes what you see.

**Sunset — the whole story in one view.** The port below, the white rebuilt city to the south, and later, from up here, you can literally see the lit wall of the souk. Then dinner at the marina or in Talborjt.

> Morning in the souk, evening on the hill — that single day is the whole arc of Agadir: destroyed 1960, rebuilt into this.
