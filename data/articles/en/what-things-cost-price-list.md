## A compass, not a contract

Prices move with season, quality and your [bargaining](/en/visit/bargaining-101) — but honest ranges exist, and knowing them before you walk in is the whole game. Indicative, 2026, in MAD:

## The rule that beats any list

**Ask the same question at three stalls.** When three independent sellers converge on a number, that's the real price — then bargain from knowledge instead of hope. And read the two red-flag rules: [fake argan under ~50 MAD/100 ml](/en/visit/argan-oil-real-or-fake) is not a bargain, it's a different liquid; and "saffron" sold cheap in generous piles is dyed corn silk.

Carry small notes. Cash is the souk's only language — and a 200 MAD note on a 40 MAD deal will mysteriously have no change.
